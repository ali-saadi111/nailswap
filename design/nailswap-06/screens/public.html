1. Who we are

NailSwap is run by NailSwap SAL, Beirut. We help you try nail designs on your own hand and book the salon that does them. Salons set their own prices and do the work.

2. Bookings and cancellations

A booking is confirmed when you get an SMS with your reference. Cancel free until 24 hours before your visit. Later cancellations or no-shows may cost up to 50% of the service.

3. Try-on photos and AI processing

Photos you upload are processed only to render your look. We delete them after 24 hours unless you save a look. Saved looks stay until you remove them.

4. Payments

You pay the salon directly unless a deposit is shown at checkout.
